# WF-019 — Missing Deal Creation Associations

**Severity:** HIGH  
**Category:** Data Integrity  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
CREATE_DEAL action lacks required contact/company associations.

## Why It Matters
deals created as orphans with no associated contacts; breaks pipeline visibility.

## How To Fix
add associationCategory fields to CREATE_DEAL action.

## Detection Logic
CREATE_DEAL action has no associations or associationCategory fields.
