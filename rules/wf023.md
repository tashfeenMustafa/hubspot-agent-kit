# WF-023 — Stale Updated-At vs Activity

**Severity:** LOW  
**Category:** Governance  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Workflow updated_at timestamp is >180 days old with recent enrollments.

## Why It Matters
suggests workflow was set-and-forgotten; may have stale logic not reflecting current process.

## How To Fix
review workflow logic; update if still valid.

## Detection Logic
updated_at > 180 days ago AND enrollment activity in last 30 days.
