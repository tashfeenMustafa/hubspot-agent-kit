# WF-001 — Zombie Workflow

**Severity:** MEDIUM  
**Category:** Performance  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Workflow is active but 0 enrollments in 90 days.

## Why It Matters
wastes automation limits, adds noise.

## How To Fix
disable/archive or fix enrollment criteria.

## Detection Logic
is_active=True AND enrollment_count_90d=0.
