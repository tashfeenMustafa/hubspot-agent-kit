# WF-026 — Long Linear Chain

**Severity:** LOW  
**Category:** Performance  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Workflow has >15 sequential actions with no branching.

## Why It Matters
hard to maintain, slow execution, no conditional logic for edge cases.

## How To Fix
refactor into branching workflow or split into sub-workflows.

## Detection Logic
action chain depth > 15 with no BRANCH type actions.
