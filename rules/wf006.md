# WF-006 — Inverted Lifecycle Stage Logic

**Severity:** CRITICAL  
**Category:** Data Integrity  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Action downgrades lifecyclestage, violating sticky rule.

## Why It Matters
contacts regress in pipeline, corrupts lifecycle reporting.

## How To Fix
remove the downgrade action; lifecycle is unidirectional in HubSpot.

## Detection Logic
action writes lifecyclestage to lead/subscriber/marketingqualifiedlead/salesqualifiedlead from a deal-triggered flow.
