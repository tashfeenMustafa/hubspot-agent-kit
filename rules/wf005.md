# WF-005 — Circular Enrollment Loop

**Severity:** CRITICAL  
**Category:** Performance  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Cycle detected in workflow-to-enrollment graph.

## Why It Matters
infinite loop, contacts stuck, automation quota consumed.

## How To Fix
break the chain by removing one enroll-in-workflow action.

## Detection Logic
workflowId references form a cycle in directed graph.
