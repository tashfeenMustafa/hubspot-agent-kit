# WF-003 — Broken List Reference

**Severity:** HIGH  
**Category:** Data Integrity  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Enrollment criteria references a deleted list.

## Why It Matters
workflow never enrolls anyone without error.

## How To Fix
remove or replace the deleted list reference.

## Detection Logic
list_filter_branch listId not in portal active lists.
