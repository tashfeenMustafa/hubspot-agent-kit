# WF-011 — Duplicate Workflow

**Severity:** MEDIUM  
**Category:** Performance  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Two or more workflows overlap on enrollment criteria and write targets.

## Why It Matters
contacts receive duplicate actions, data overwritten unpredictably.

## How To Fix
consolidate into a single workflow with branching logic.

## Detection Logic
requires full portal scan; cross-workflow enrollment+action overlap analysis.
