# WF-036 — Owner Assignment Missing Rotation

**Severity:** MEDIUM  
**Category:** Configuration  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Round-robin owner assignment action not configured, assigning all leads to single owner.

## Why It Matters
uneven lead distribution; one rep overwhelmed, others idle.

## How To Fix
configure rotation logic in the owner assignment action.

## Detection Logic
ASSIGN_OWNER action with rotation=false or missing rotation configuration.
