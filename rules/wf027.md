# WF-027 — SAL/SQL Stage Mismatch

**Severity:** HIGH  
**Category:** Data Integrity  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Workflow sets dealstage to SQL but does not update lifecyclestage to salesqualifiedlead (or vice versa).

## Why It Matters
deal stage and lifecycle stage diverge, breaking pipeline reporting and SLA tracking.

## How To Fix
always update both dealstage and lifecyclestage together in stage-change workflows.

## Detection Logic
action sets dealstage=qualifiedtobuy without also setting lifecyclestage=salesqualifiedlead.
