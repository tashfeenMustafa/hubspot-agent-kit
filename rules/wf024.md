# WF-024 — Empty Enrollment Criteria

**Severity:** HIGH  
**Category:** Configuration  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Workflow has no enrollment criteria defined.

## Why It Matters
workflow never enrolls anyone, or enrolls everyone — both are wrong.

## How To Fix
define specific enrollment criteria matching intended audience.

## Detection Logic
enrollment_trigger has no filters and no list references.
