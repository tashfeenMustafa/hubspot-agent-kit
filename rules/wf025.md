# WF-025 — Missing Owner On Orphan-Prone Action

**Severity:** MEDIUM  
**Category:** Data Integrity  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Assign-owner or rotate-owner action has no fallback owner defined.

## Why It Matters
contacts assigned to deleted/deactivated users fall into void.

## How To Fix
add a fallback owner to all owner-assignment actions.

## Detection Logic
ASSIGN_OWNER action type with no fallback_owner_id field.
