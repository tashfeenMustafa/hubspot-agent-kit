# WF-018 — Meeting Deal Missing Activity Type Filter

**Severity:** HIGH  
**Category:** Data Quality  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Deal-creation-on-meeting workflow lacks hs_activity_type filter.

## Why It Matters
ghost deals created for non-sales meetings (service calls, internal syncs).

## How To Fix
add hs_activity_type=Commercial Call - Prospect filter to enrollment criteria.

## Detection Logic
workflow has CREATE_DEAL action AND trigger lacks hs_activity_type filter.
