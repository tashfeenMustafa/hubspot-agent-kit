# WF-033 — Blank Meeting Outcomes Block SAL

**Severity:** HIGH  
**Category:** Data Quality  
**API Fixable:** Yes  
**HubSpot Tier Required:** None

## What It Detects
Meeting outcome field is blank/unset, blocking SAL deal creation workflow from firing.

## Why It Matters
qualified meetings not converted to deals; revenue opportunity lost silently.

## How To Fix
ensure all meeting records have hs_meeting_outcome set via backfill script or form defaults.

## Detection Logic
deal-creation workflow trigger requires hs_meeting_outcome AND portal has >0 meetings with blank outcome.
